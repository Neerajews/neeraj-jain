01 / ABOUT

Research, teaching and academic leadership.

I am an Associate Professor at Bennett University with a Ph.D. in Information Technology from IIIT Allahabad.

My research spans Federated Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things, Wireless Sensor Networks, localization, clustering and intelligent networked systems. Alongside research, I contribute to student mentoring, curriculum development, academic coordination and technology-enabled learning.

04 / PROJECTS & INFRASTRUCTURE

Applied research and academic infrastructure.

Research work complemented by hands-on laboratory development and technology-enabled learning.

Research Project · 2024

Early Warning System for Hydro Power Plant

Completed under the MoU between JP Power Ventures and Jaypee Universities. The project represents an applied research initiative focused on early-warning capability for a hydro-power-plant environment, bringing together monitoring, sensing and intelligent computing concepts for timely identification of potentially significant operating conditions.

Academic Infrastructure · JIIT Noida

Establishment of Internet of Things Laboratory

Set up an Internet of Things laboratory at the JIIT Sector 128 campus, Noida. The initiative supported practical exposure to IoT concepts and provided an academic environment for hands-on work related to connected devices, sensing, embedded/Arduino programming and IoT-oriented experimentation.
